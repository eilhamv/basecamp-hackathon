# Proposal to Vandelay Logistics: Fleet & Operations Data Platform

## Executive summary
- **Total cost: €1,980,000 for the full 4-year term,** net of an 18% discount, fixed with no escalators.
- Exadata and SAP BW are switched off by **November 2027** in a dated, three-phase rollout.
- 24/7 support, with a **P1 response within 30 minutes**.

## Rollout plan
| Phase | Months | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | Aug–Oct 2026 | EU regions live; telematics streaming at 60K events/s |
| 2. Migration | Nov 2026–Jun 2027 | SAP S/4HANA, TMS and WMS sources moved; Tableau cut over |
| 3. Switch-off | Jul–Nov 2027 | Oracle Exadata and SAP BW switched off by 30 Nov 2027 |

## Exit plan
At end of term or on termination, all data and the catalog are exported as Iceberg tables to Vandelay's own S3 buckets. This takes 21 days and is **at our cost**, followed by certified deletion.

## Proof before signing
- A reference call with Initech Sensors, a comparable streaming-IoT customer.
- A 45-day pilot on one country's fleet data before signature.

## Support
24/7 follow-the-sun support. P1 response in 30 minutes or less; P2 in 2 hours.
