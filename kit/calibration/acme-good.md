# Proposal to Acme Corp: Enterprise Data Platform

## Executive summary
- **Total contract value: $2,640,000 for the full 5 years** (3-year term plus 2-year renewal), net of a 22% discount, fixed with no escalators.
- A dated, three-phase rollout that decommissions Teradata by **December 2027**.
- Proof before you sign: a reference call with Initech Sensors and a 60-day proof-of-concept.

## Power BI
Native Power BI integration through our certified DirectQuery adapter, our most mature BI connector, for all 600 users.

## Rollout plan
| Phase | Months | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | Jul–Sep 2026 | EU primary region live, IoT streaming ingest at 80K events/s |
| 2. Migration | Oct 2026–Jun 2027 | 30+ batch sources moved, Power BI cut over |
| 3. Decommission | Jul–Dec 2027 | Teradata warehouses switched off by 31 Dec 2027 |

## Exit plan
If the contract ends or is terminated, all Acme data and metadata is exported in open formats (Delta and Parquet files plus catalog export). Delivery is within 30 days of notice, **at our cost**, with written confirmation of deletion afterwards.

## Proof before signing
- Reference call with Initech Sensors (industrial IoT), a comparable customer live since 2025.
- A 60-day proof-of-concept on Acme's own sensor data, with the fee credited to Year 1.

## Contract approach
- Liability is capped at 24 months of fees, with a separate data-breach super-cap.
- Audits on 30 days' notice, once a year.
- Termination for convenience on 90 days' notice.
